    03 / Principles

    How I think about design

    01

    Clarity over decoration

    Interfaces should reduce cognitive load before adding visual personality.

    02

    Systems over screens

    A product should not be designed one screen at a time.

    03

    Constraints are design material

    Complex requirements often reveal the real design problem.

    04

    AI should extend thinking

    AI should accelerate exploration, iteration and implementation — not replace design judgment.

    05 / Journey

    ECE → UX → Product → Systems → AI

    A career that moved from circuits to interfaces to the systems behind them.

    1. 01

      ECE

      An Electronics & Communication Engineering background — signals, constraints and systems described in diagrams before interfaces.

    2. 02

      Exploring software & product

      Moving from hardware thinking to software products, and noticing that the interesting problems were about people using the thing.

    3. 03

      UI/UX design internship

      First structured product work: interface design, prototyping and agile collaboration inside a delivery rhythm.

    4. 04

      Associate Designer

      Current product design role — enterprise interfaces, UX flows and design decisions that have to survive real requirements.

    5. 05

      Design systems

      Components, variants, tokens, documentation and accessibility — designing the thing that makes other design possible.

    6. 06

      AI × Design

      Using AI-assisted workflows to shorten the distance between an idea, an interface and a working prototype.
